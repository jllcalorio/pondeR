# pondeR: Making Statistical Analysis a Little Bit Easier

Generate publication-ready statistical tables and figures with minimal
code. Provides descriptive summary tables, automatic selection of
group-difference and association tests with effect sizes and post hoc
comparisons, correlation analysis, receiver operating characteristic
curves, logistic regression with optional Firth correction (Firth, 1993)
[doi:10.1093/biomet/80.1.27](https://doi.org/10.1093/biomet/80.1.27) ,
penalized regression (Friedman, Hastie and Tibshirani, 2010)
[doi:10.18637/jss.v033.i01](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v033.i01) ,
linear mixed-effects models (Bates et al., 2015)
[doi:10.18637/jss.v067.i01](https://doi.org/10.18637/jss.v067.i01) ,
principal component and principal coordinate analysis with permutational
multivariate analysis of variance (Anderson, 2001)
[doi:10.1111/j.1442-9993.2001.01070.pp.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1442-9993.2001.01070.pp.x)
, partial least squares, linear discriminant analysis effect size
(Segata et al., 2011)
[doi:10.1186/gb-2011-12-6-r60](https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2011-12-6-r60)
, content validity indices (Lawshe, 1975)
[doi:10.1111/j.1744-6570.1975.tb01393.x](https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1744-6570.1975.tb01393.x)
, World Health Organization child growth z-scores, and plotting helpers
for volcano, score, scree, heatmap, dendrogram, odds-ratio and
relative-abundance plots.
